Privacy Policy for Shayari Lok

Package ID: com.cyberking.shayarilok • Effective Date: 2026

1. No Account or Personal Data Collection

Shayari Lok is an ultra-lightweight reading application that works without user accounts, registration, or login. We do not collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.

2. Local Offline Cache & Favorites

Your favorite Shayari selections and the latest synced Shayari content are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences solely to provide instant offline access. Clearing the app data or uninstalling the app removes this local data completely.

3. Network Requests

Shayari Lok uses standard network connectivity (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) only to fetch public read-only Shayari content from the configured remote JSON file. No user tracking identifiers are transmitted.

4. Third-Party Links

The app may contain optional links to external community channels or app store updates. Visiting external websites is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the official community channel listed inside the app's Settings screen.
