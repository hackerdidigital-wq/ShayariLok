Privacy Policy for Shayari Lok

Developer: CyberKing Digital • Package ID: com.cyberking.shayarilok • Effective Date: 2026

1. Google Sign-In & Account Information

Shayari Lok uses Google Sign-In with Firebase Authentication so members can securely access the Shayari Lok community. When you sign in, we process your Google account user ID, display name, email address, and Google profile photo URL. Your email address is stored privately in your user document and used to generate your unique deterministic community ID (e.g. @SL_1234).

2. User Profile & Gallery Photo Uploads

In the Profile tab, you may voluntarily edit your display name, @SL_ handle, bio, city, and address. City, bio, handle, display name, and follower/following/like counts are visible to signed-in community members for discovery and connection, while your email and address remain in your private user record. If you choose a gallery photo, Shayari Lok uses the zero-permission Android Photo Picker (without requesting broad device storage permissions) and compresses the selected image into a JPEG Data URI synced to your profile.

3. Real-Time 1-on-1 Chat & Social Notifications

Private 1-on-1 messages sent inside Shayari Lok are stored in Cloud Firestore chat rooms restricted by security rules so only the two conversation participants can read or send messages in that room. Follow, like, and message alerts are delivered only to the recipient's notification feed.

4. Admin Panel Config Sync & Local Offline Cache

Shayari Lok synchronizes app configuration, Shayari collections, announcements, promotional banners, FAQs, polls, and privacy notices from Cloud Firestore and the Aegis Enterprise Console SDK endpoint. Favorites, reading preferences, language/country choices, 18+ safety toggles, daily reading streaks, and offline caches are stored locally via Android SharedPreferences.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

When ads are enabled via remote configuration, Shayari Lok displays banner and interstitial advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), which may process device advertising identifiers and ad interaction signals in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Your Controls, Links & Contact

You can update or clear your profile details and avatar photo anytime, toggle 18+ Safe Mode, or sign out via Settings or Profile. External links inside promotional banners or updates open in third-party apps or browsers governed by their own privacy policies. For privacy questions or account assistance, contact CyberKing Digital via the official community channel listed in Settings & Info.
